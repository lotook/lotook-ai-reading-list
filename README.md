# Lotook AI Reading List

A curated, plain-language reading list for people who want to keep up with AI without the hype, maintained alongside [Lotook](https://lotook.com/), an independent publication with AI news, insights and practical guides.

## How Lotook organizes AI coverage
- **AI News**: recent model, product and research developments, explained with context and links to original sources.
- **AI Insights**: analysis of how AI is changing technology, business and work.
- **AI Guides**: step-by-step guides, for example choosing an AI model or fact-checking AI answers.

## Habits for following AI news well
1. Read the primary source (paper, release notes, official blog) before the commentary.
2. Separate what a model can do today from what was announced or demoed.
3. Check the date: AI facts go stale quickly.
4. Verify AI-generated answers against at least one independent source before relying on them.
5. Try tools yourself on a small, real task before adopting them at work.

## Questions to ask about any new AI tool
- What data does it see, and where is it stored?
- What happens when it is wrong, and who checks?
- Does it save time on a task you actually do every week?
